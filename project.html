Our Community Service Project

Creating Career Awareness for a Better Future

Project Overview

Our Community Service Project focuses on creating awareness among 10th class students about the different educational and career opportunities available after completing their secondary education. Through this initiative, we aim to provide useful information about Intermediate courses, Diploma, ITI, Vocational Education, and other career pathways. Our objective is to help students understand their interests, explore available opportunities, and make informed decisions about their future.

Project Objectives

📢

Create Awareness

Spread awareness about educational opportunities after 10th.

📖

Provide Information

Explain different courses, eligibility, duration, and career paths.

🧭

Guide Students

Help students understand their interests and strengths.

✅

Better Decisions

Support students in making informed educational choices.
