Explore Career Opportunities After 10th

Completing your 10th class opens the door to many educational pathways. Explore the options below and understand which path suits your interests.

📐

Intermediate – MPC

Full Form: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry

Suitable for students interested in mathematics, science, technology, and engineering.

  • Duration: 2 Years
  • Eligibility: Passed 10th Standard
🧬

Intermediate – BiPC

Full Form: Biology, Physics, Chemistry

Suitable for students interested in biology, healthcare, and life sciences.

  • Duration: 2 Years
  • Eligibility: Passed 10th Standard
📊

Intermediate – CEC

Full Form: Civics, Economics, Commerce

Helps students understand business, economics, society, and commerce.

  • Duration: 2 Years
  • Eligibility: Passed 10th Standard
📈

Intermediate – MEC

Full Form: Mathematics, Economics, Commerce

Suitable for students interested in mathematics, economics, finance, and business.

  • Duration: 2 Years
  • Eligibility: Passed 10th Standard
⚙️

Diploma / Polytechnic

Provides technical and practical education in engineering and technical fields.

  • Duration: Usually 3 Years
  • Eligibility: Passed 10th (Entrance Exam based)
🛠️

Industrial Training Institute (ITI)

Practical and job-oriented technical training to develop specific industry skills.

  • Duration: 1 to 2 Years
  • Eligibility: Passed 10th Standard
💼

Vocational Courses

Focused on immediate practical skills and industry-related operational training.

  • Duration: 1 to 2 Years
  • Eligibility: Passed 10th Standard
🚀

Skill Development Programs

Short supplementary courses to build specialized technical and digital capabilities.

  • Duration: 3 to 6 Months
  • Eligibility: Open to all students
