About Our Team

We are students working on a Community Service Project to spread awareness about educational opportunities and career pathways among 10th class students. Through this project, we aim to make career guidance accessible and understandable for students.

Our Mission

To help students make informed decisions about their educational and career future by providing clear, accurate, and comprehensive guidance.

Our Vision

To create awareness and ensure that every student understands the opportunities available after 10th class, bridging the gap for rural and government school students.
